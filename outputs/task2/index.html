Small acts, real impact

Make room for reefs to thrive.

You don't have to live near the water to help. Pick one change that fits your everyday life.

01

Choose sun protection thoughtfully

Wear sun-protective clothing and check local advice on sunscreen ingredients.

02

Keep plastic out of the water

03

Back trusted groups and policies that protect ocean habitats and coastal communities.

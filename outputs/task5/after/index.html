01✳

The reef itself

Coral is an animal

Corals are tiny animals called polyps. Many live together in colonies and build hard structures that can form a reef over a long time.

↳ Reefs are living habitats, not rocks.

02≈

Life in the blue

A busy underwater home

Fish, crabs, mollusks, sea turtles, and many other organisms use reef habitats for food, shelter, or places to grow.

↳ Many lives are connected here.

03!

Pressures reefs face

Small changes matter

Warmer seas can stress corals. Pollution, damaging fishing practices, and careless contact can also harm reef life.

↳ Reducing harm helps give reefs a chance.
